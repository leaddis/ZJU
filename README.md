# ZJU Coursework and Research Projects

This repository contains a collection of my coursework, course projects, research projects, and undergraduate thesis completed during my studies at Zhejiang University (ZJU).

## Repository Contents

The repository includes work from the following courses and projects:

### Coursework

- **Numerical Analysis**  
  Programming assignments and numerical methods for solving mathematical and computational problems.

- **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)**  
  Programming exercises and projects focused on object-oriented design, implementation, and software development.

- **Practical Optimization Algorithms**  
  Implementations and assignments involving numerical optimization, mathematical modeling, and optimization algorithms.

- **Quantitative Investment**  
  Course materials and projects related to asset pricing, portfolio analysis, financial data analysis, and quantitative investment strategies.

- **Quantum Computing**  
  Coursework covering the mathematical foundations, algorithms, and computational principles of quantum computing.

- **Statistical Learning**  
  Projects and assignments involving statistical modeling, machine learning, regression, classification, and data analysis.

- **Curve Fitting Project with Mathematica**  
  A curve-fitting project involving mathematical modeling, spline methods, numerical approximation, and visualization.

- **Topological Data Analysis**  
  Coursework and implementations involving topology-based methods for analyzing complex and high-dimensional data.

### Undergraduate Thesis

- **ISVD-PDE**  
  My undergraduate thesis project, focusing on numerical methods and computational techniques involving incremental singular value decomposition and partial differential equations.

### Extracurricular Research

- **TreeImagine**  
  An extracurricular research project exploring tree-based representations and computational methods for image generation and analysis.

## Purpose

This repository serves as:

- An archive of my academic work at Zhejiang University
- A record of my development in mathematics, computer science, data science, and scientific computing
- A portfolio of selected coursework, research implementations, reports, and presentations

Some folders may contain experimental code, course-specific implementations, reports, datasets, or presentation materials. Individual projects may not be actively maintained or intended for production use.
